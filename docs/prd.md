# Product Requirements Document: AI-Powered E-commerce MVP

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the product requirements for a Proof-of-Concept (POC) e-commerce platform designed to showcase the value and functionality of two core AI-powered features: AI Review Summarization and an Intelligent Cross-Selling Engine. The primary objective is to demonstrate the potential of these AI enhancements within an e-commerce context, rather than to build a fully commercial, feature-complete online store.

## 2. Vision and Goal

The vision is to explore how AI can enhance the online shopping experience. The goal of this POC is to validate the effectiveness of AI-driven features in improving user engagement and product discovery.

## 3. Problem Statement

Online shoppers are often overwhelmed by a large number of product reviews, making it difficult to make informed decisions quickly. Additionally, they may miss out on relevant products that would complement their purchase. This project aims to solve these problems by providing concise, AI-generated review summaries and intelligent product recommendations.

## 4. Target Audience

The primary target audience for this MVP is **students**. They are typically tech-savvy, open to new technologies, and represent a demographic that frequently shops online.

## 5. Features and Scope (MVP)

### 5.1. AI Review Summarization

*   **Functionality:** An AI model will process and summarize product reviews to provide customers with a quick, digestible overview of product feedback.
*   **Data Bootstrapping:** To ensure the feature is demonstrable at launch, initial product reviews will be generated by an AI and pre-populated in the database.
*   **User Feedback Mechanism:** Users can provide feedback on each summary via a simple "like" or "dislike" button.
    *   A "dislike" will hide the summary for that specific user.
    *   All feedback will be logged for future model analysis and tuning.
*   **Feature Flag Control:** The entire feature will be managed by a feature flag, allowing it to be enabled or disabled at an administrative level without requiring a new deployment.

### 5.2. Intelligent Cross-Selling Engine

*   **Functionality:** The system will leverage embeddings of the product catalog to analyze items and provide relevant cross-sell recommendations.
*   **Placement:** Recommendations will be displayed to the user on the checkout page to encourage further product discovery.

### 5.3. Standard E-commerce Platform

*   **Authentication:** Access to the platform will be restricted to registered users (i.e., a "closed beta"). All users must create an account and log in to browse products and use the platform's features.
*   **Product Catalog:** The store will be populated with a sample catalog of approximately 6-8 product categories, with around 10 items in each category.
*   **Core E-commerce Functionality:**
    *   **Shopping Cart:** Users can add products to and manage their shopping cart.
    *   **Item Filtering:** Basic filtering options will be available for the product catalog.
*   **Mocked Checkout:** A simulated checkout process will be implemented to provide a complete user journey without involving real financial transactions.

## 6. User Stories

*   **As a student shopper, I want to quickly understand the general sentiment of product reviews, so that I can make a purchase decision faster.**
*   **As a student shopper, I want to see if a review summary is helpful, so I can provide feedback to improve the system.**
*   **As a student shopper, I want to discover other products that are relevant to what I'm buying, so I can make sure I have everything I need.**
*   **As a new user, I want to create an account, so that I can access the store and its features.**
*   **As a logged-in user, I want to browse products by category and add them to my cart, so that I can proceed to checkout.**

## 7. Technical and Operational Requirements

*   **AI Services Integration:** The platform will integrate with third-party AI services (e.g., OpenAI) to power the summarization and cross-selling features.
*   **API Rate Limiting:** To manage costs and prevent abuse, rate limiting will be implemented on a per-IP basis using Redis.
*   **Data & Privacy Policy:** All users must accept a data policy upon registration. The policy will clearly state that their public product reviews will be used to train and operate the AI models.

## 8. Success Metrics (MVP)

The primary success of the MVP will be measured by direct user feedback and engagement with the core AI features.

*   **AI Review Summarization:**
    *   **Primary KPI:** The like-to-dislike ratio on the generated summaries. A high ratio indicates that users find the summaries valuable.
*   **Intelligent Cross-Selling Engine:**
    *   **Primary KPI:** User engagement with the recommendations presented during the mock checkout process. This will be measured by:
        *   Click-Through Rate (CTR) on recommended products.
        *   Conversion rate (items added to cart from recommendations).

## 9. Out of Scope for MVP

The following features and functionalities are explicitly out of scope for this initial POC:

*   Real payment processing and any financial transactions.
*   Guest checkout or anonymous user access.
*   Advanced content moderation or real-time correction of potentially inaccurate AI-generated content.
*   Complex user profiles, detailed order history, order tracking, and multiple shipping options.
